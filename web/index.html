psxel.

A PlayStation-style renderer in Rust: fixed-point GTE, ordering tables, 1 MB of VRAM with 4-bit CLUT textures and a software rasteriser that copies the original GPU's affine texturing, dithering and blend modes. Runs in the browser via WebGPU or WebGL.

PSX Rally

An endless procedurally generated rally stage with a co-driver, five evolving biomes, day and night, weather, fords, ravines, tunnels and a 1970s Mk1 Escort in twelve period liveries.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD, space for handbrake, R to reset. Touch: drag up to accelerate, sideways to steer, second finger for handbrake. Gamepad supported.

Jungle Temple

A looping fly-through of an overgrown stepped pyramid: canopy, waterfall and pool, through the falls into a hidden crystal cave, up into the cave the falls pour from, with its indoor pool and sunken treasure, and down a passage to a ruin in a sinkhole, then through carved torch-lit corridors to an idol under a shaft of light, down into a flooded crypt, and up to a brazier-lit shrine. More treasure is hidden for the free camera to find. Positional ambience for the jungle, falls, torches and chamber.

Space or tap to pause, C for a free camera (WASD, arrows, Q/E to fly), V to toggle the CRT filter, G for short fog, F for a period 20 fps frame limit, M to mute.

Wreck Dive

Pilot a little yellow ROV on its tether down a reef wall to the wreck of the S.S. Oceanic. Light fades from turquoise to navy with depth, caustics ripple over the sand, the hold still has its cargo, and something glows at the bottom of the trench.

WASD or arrows to drive, Q/E or Space/Shift to dive and climb, L for lamps, V for the CRT filter, F for 20 fps, M to mute. Touch: drag to drive, two fingers to dive. Gamepad supported.

Presenter test card: the first milestone, checking VRAM upload and integer-scaled presentation.
